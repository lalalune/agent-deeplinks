# Brand SVG assets

The SVGs identify their respective clients in the link buttons. Names and marks belong to their owners and are subject to applicable brand guidelines. The project's MIT license covers its own code.

Each report records the origin of the assets it covers:

- [ChatGPT, Codex, Claude, and Claude Code](../research/openai-anthropic.md)
- [Cursor, Antigravity, T3 Code, OpenCode, and ZCode](../research/coding-agents.md)
- [Grok, Grok Bot, Meta Muse, Kimi, Hermes Agent, and OpenClaw](../research/other-agents.md)
- Gemini uses the original vector spark from the footer of Google's [Gemini desktop page](https://gemini.google/desktop/).
- Perplexity uses the geometric brand mark from [Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/icons/perplexity.svg).
